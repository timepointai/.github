# TIMEPOINT IS THE SYNTHETIC TIME MACHINE FOR SYNTHETIC TIME TRAVEL.

Timepoint is a synthetic time machine: it simulates a decisive moment, in the future or from history, so you can step in, change what happens and see where it leads.

## Three moves

- **TRAVEL FORWARD**: simulate what comes next: a question, a meeting, a decision.
- **TRAVEL BACK**: start from the outcome you want and walk back to the moves that reach it, or visit a moment from history.
- **CHANGE WHAT HAPPENS**: take a seat in the moment and play it.

Keep score against what really happens.
